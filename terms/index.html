JARVIS terms and privacy

JARVIS is a personal assistant that runs on its owner's own computer, for the owner's use only. It is not offered to anyone else.

When you connect it through MyChart, it reads your health record so it can answer your questions about it: test results, vitals, medications, conditions, allergies, immunizations, visits, reports, visit notes and your care team. It only reads. It never changes anything in your record.

What it reads stays on that computer, except for the parts it sends to Anthropic's Claude to work out an answer. Nothing is sold, and nothing is shared with anyone else.

You can cut off its access at any time from MyChart, under the apps that have access to your account.
